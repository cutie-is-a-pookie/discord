# 💬 What is Discord?

Discord is a communication platform made for communities, friends, creators, and developers.

It lets people communicate through **text, voice, and video**, while also providing tools to build and manage online communities.

## 🌐 How Discord Works

Discord is organized into **servers**. A server is a community where people can chat and interact.

Inside a server, you'll usually find:

- 💬 **Text Channels** — Send messages, images, files, and links.
- 🔊 **Voice Channels** — Talk with other members using voice.
- 🎥 **Video Calls** — Chat through video and screen sharing.
- 🛡️ **Roles & Permissions** — Control what members can access.
- 🤖 **Bots & Apps** — Add custom features and automation.
- 📢 **Announcements** — Share important updates with a community.

## 🤖 Discord Bots

One of the coolest parts of Discord is its developer ecosystem.

Developers can create **Discord bots and apps** that interact with servers and users.

Bots can do things like:

- ⚡ Automate tasks
- 🛡️ Moderate servers
- 🎮 Add games and fun commands
- 📊 Track statistics
- 🎵 Play music
- 🔧 Provide custom tools
